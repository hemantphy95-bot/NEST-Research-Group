Research Projects

Ongoing and completed research projects reflecting our work in ion-beam modification, plasmonic nanomaterials, functional materials, and sustainable energy technologies.



Ongoing Projects

Ongoing

Plasmonic Nanoparticles in SiO₂ and TiO₂ Matrices: Exploring Nonlinear Optical Properties for Optical Limiting Applications

Funded by the UGC–DAE Consortium for Scientific Research (CSR) under the CRS — Collaborative Research Scheme (UGC-DAE CSR). This project investigates nonlinear optical properties of plasmonic nanoparticles embedded in oxide matrices, with the goal of developing efficient optical limiting materials for photonic protection applications.

Role: Principal Investigator

2025 – 2026 (Ongoing)

Ongoing

Enhanced Photoelectrocatalysis of Anodes in Alkaline Electrolyser via Ion Beam Irradiation for Green Hydrogen Production

Funded by the Inter-University Accelerator Centre (IUAC), New Delhi under the UFR Scheme — User Facility Research Scheme (IUAC). The project focuses on ion-beam-induced modification of catalytic anodes to enhance photoelectrocatalytic performance for sustainable green hydrogen production.

Role: Co-Principal Investigator

2025 – Ongoing



Completed Projects

Completed

Unveiling the Temperature-Dependent Growth Mechanism of Silica-Embedded AuAg and AgPt Nanoparticles

Seed Money Project funded by Devi Ahilya Vishwavidyalaya (DAVV), Indore. The project investigated nucleation, alloy formation, and temperature-dependent structural evolution of silica-embedded bimetallic nanoparticles using advanced microscopy and synchrotron-based techniques.

Role: Principal Investigator
